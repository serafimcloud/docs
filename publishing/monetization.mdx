Most monetization on this page works the same way: you drop in a link to a service you already use (a checkout page, a booking page, a donation platform), and 21st.dev surfaces it to the right audience at the right moment. The one exception is selling a template on 21st itself: for those listings, checkout, delivery, and your payout are handled here (see [Get Paid by 21st](#get-paid-by-21st)). This page covers all of it.

<Info>
  **Components stay free.** There's no price field on individual components —
  monetization applies to **templates** (one-time paid listings) and to blocks
  on your **public profile** (hire, donate, pro catalog). If you want to sell a
  component-like product, package it as a template instead.
</Info>

## Sell a Template

Templates are the only catalog item that supports a price. Each template has its own **Price** and **Buy / download URL**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your template">
    In Studio, go to your **Templates** list and open (or create) the listing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a price">
    Enter a **Price (USD)**. Leave it at `0` to keep the template free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a buy link">
    Set **Buy / download URL** to wherever the purchase actually happens — your
    own Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy/Stripe Payment Link, or a page on your own site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **A listing you create is bought on your own site.** You set a price and a
  **Buy / download URL**; a visitor who clicks buy sees a disclaimer confirming
  they're leaving 21st.dev, then lands on your storefront. You're responsible
  for delivery, receipts, and refunds. To sell with checkout on 21st instead,
  see [Get Paid by 21st](#get-paid-by-21st) below.
</Warning>

If your buy link already carries an affiliate/referral parameter for a third-party vendor (for example a template marketplace that pays you `?via=yourname`), set that same URL as your **Pro components link** (see below) — 21st.dev automatically appends your referral parameter to that template's buy link so you still get credit.

## Get Paid by 21st

Some templates are sold on 21st.dev itself: card checkout on the template page, instant download, no redirect. For these listings 21st handles the payment and pays you your share.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the code">
    Attach your template's code to the listing: a zip in the Studio editor, or
    `21st template publish` from the CLI. Hosted delivery is what makes
    on-site checkout possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Price it">
    The minimum price is **$3**. As you type a price, the editor shows exactly
    what you receive per sale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get released for sale">
    After review, listings are released for sale individually. This is not
    self-serve yet: write to support@21st.dev to have your template
    considered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Your share.** You receive **70% of what's left after the card fee**; on a $39 sale that's $26.30. The split is locked in at purchase time, so a later price change never restates a sale already made.

**Payouts.** Each sale's share stays on hold for **30 days** (the refund and chargeback window), then joins your available balance. Once the cleared balance reaches **$50** it's paid out in **USDC**, on **Base** or **Polygon**. Set the wallet in **Studio → Earnings**: paste the address, hold to confirm it, pick a network. The same page lists every sale (who bought, when it clears, what came back) and every payout with its transaction link.

**Refunds.** A refund or chargeback returns the buyer's money, revokes their download access, and removes that sale's share from your balance.

Buyers keep lifetime access to what they bought and can re-download any time from **Settings → Purchases**.

## Components Are Free-Only

There is currently no price field, checkout, or paid tier for individual components — only templates carry a price. If you want to monetize component-style work, publish the free version to build reach, and link your paid catalog using the **Pro components** block below.

Component **visibility** is separate from selling. Published components remain
free to visitors. Private personal components remain visible only to their
owner, while private components in an active Team workspace are available to
its members. Private content is not a paid listing and has no customer checkout.

## "Work with Me" Block

A hire-and-contact card that renders on your public profile (`21st.dev/@you`). Configure it in **Studio → Profile → Work with me**.

Fields (all optional — the block only appears once at least one is filled in):

| Field                | What it does                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Headline**         | Defaults to "Work with me" if left blank.                                                                                                           |
| **Pitch**            | One short paragraph under the headline (280 chars), e.g. "Available for freelance and contract work."                                               |
| **Hire link**        | A booking or contact link — cal.com, Calendly, your contact form, etc.                                                                              |
| **Button label**     | Overrides the CTA button text. Defaults to "Work with me".                                                                                          |
| **Contact email**    | Adds a separate Email button.                                                                                                                       |
| **Right-side media** | Choose **None**, a built-in **preset** graphic, an uploaded **image/GIF**, or one of your own **published components** to display beside the block. |

## Get Tipped or Sponsored

A "Support" block for one-time tips or recurring sponsorship. Configure it in **Studio → Profile → Donation** by pasting a **Donation link**.

Only links to known donation platforms are accepted:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="GitHub Sponsors" />
  <Card title="Buy Me a Coffee" />
  <Card title="Ko-fi" />
  <Card title="Patreon" />
  <Card title="PayPal / PayPal.me" />
  <Card title="Open Collective" />
</CardGroup>

Liberapay, Boosty, Stripe Payment Links (`buy.stripe.com` / `donate.stripe.com`), and Lemon Squeezy links are also accepted even though they're not called out in the field's helper text.

<Info>
  When a visitor clicks **Support**, they see your avatar, bio, and stats, then
  confirm a disclaimer ("21st doesn't process payments or issue refunds") before
  being redirected to your donation platform in a new tab. The tip itself
  happens entirely on that platform — 21st.dev only surfaces the link and shows
  a click count in your Studio analytics.
</Info>

## Link Your Premium Catalog ("Pro Components")

If you sell components or templates elsewhere — your own shop, a template marketplace, a Gumroad store — add that link under **Studio → Profile → Pro components**. It renders as a **"Pro components"** block on your profile pointing at your premium catalog.

<Info>
  The profile block named **Pro components** is an outbound link to your own
  premium catalog. It is not tied to a 21st plan and does not change who can
  view your published 21st components.
</Info>

<Tip>
  If this URL includes a referral/affiliate query parameter (e.g.
  `?via=yourname`), 21st.dev reuses it: any affiliate parameter found in your
  Pro components link is automatically appended to your own templates' buy
  links, so a template sale you drive through 21st.dev still credits your
  referral.
</Tip>

## Arrange the Blocks

**Studio → Profile → Layout & visibility** controls which of the four profile blocks (**Featured**, **Work with me**, **Pro components**, **Donation**) are shown and in what order. Toggle any block off if you'd rather not display it, and drag to reorder — for example, put **Work with me** above **Donation** if you'd rather visitors hire you before they tip you.

## What's Not There (Yet)

- No price/checkout on individual components — templates only.
- The profile blocks (hire, donation, pro catalog) never move money through 21st: each of those flows ends at a service you control. Templates sold on 21st are the exception, paying out as described in [Get Paid by 21st](#get-paid-by-21st).
- Builder and Team subscriptions pay for 21st access. Builder can enable AI with a monthly credit allowance, and Team Builder + AI seats include monthly generation credits. These plans are not a way for authors to receive money. Use the **Donation** block or an external premium catalog for that.
